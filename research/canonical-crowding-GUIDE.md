This isolated proposal is not final adoption or native qualification. Original financial review and five actual source-bound account comparisons are required before integration/export/initialization. The registered economics and edge specs remain unchanged. Main and the frozen original sources are separate.

The prepared rule is `2026-10-03-atr-stop-crowding-interaction-v1`. It preserves SMA30/40/50, completed ATR protection, installed stop floors, fill ownership, finite sessions, recovery and original cash/rounding rules. Only genuine new BUY spend changes: halve once when settled funding >.0003 AND paired prior UTC daily futures/Spot trade-close basis >.01 AND completed close <= the close five completed days earlier. Missing required causal data blocks that BUY. Safety sells, held quantities and protection remain independent of these inputs. The historical adapter calls the same predicate after the ATR-only decision helper, preventing a second half-size application.

Any state carrying the previous rule, research identity or incompatible pending allocations rejects before Lifecycle recovery, including flat accounts. There is no automatic migration, reset or proof of flatness from a new directory. ## Public inputs

Native `Binance.crowding_features()` collects official public BTCUSDT `fundingRate` and paired Spot/futures `klines?interval=1d`. It never requests predicted funding or premiumIndex and never uses the finite historical feature file as a future feed. Three bounded requests refresh no more than once per minute and respect the session stop callback; failures explicitly make the relevant feature unavailable. No private futures client, account, position or order is introduced.

`spotquant.crowding.ObservedFeatures` accepts records with category (`funding`, `spot_bars`, `futures_bars`), official URL, request/receipt milliseconds, raw SHA-256 and parsed response body. Funding must identify BTCUSDT and its actual settlement timestamp/rate; eligible availability is settlement+28800000ms and age>=28800000ms is stale. Basis uses equal UTC completion boundaries (`closeTime+1`), paired positive trade closes, completion+60000ms availability, current UTC availability date and maximum one-day age. A running bar never supplies basis. Actual receipts must precede the decision and be at most one minute old. Negative settled rates are preserved.

Forward raw categories are `bars` (Spot), `futures_bars`, and `crowding_funding`. Every response retains exact raw bytes, URL, request/receipt clocks, raw hash and path. HTTP failures retain the actual error response bytes with `error` and `http_status`; a transport failure has empty raw bytes and explicit error metadata. The source maps these records into the same executable contract. Decisions include `crowding` diagnostics containing input provenance/missing cause, completed trend references, scale, resulting rounded quote, diagnostic risk scale and blocking reason. Audit reconstructs decisions from the retained bytes. Fresh FRED CSV/metadata, first-warmup and genuinely later-interval requirements are unchanged; no retrospective backfill or unattended-operation promise is added.

The observed HTTP451 restrictions may leave actual initialization market-pending. They are not bypassed. Native and paper execution remain unqualified, actual native account-days zero.
