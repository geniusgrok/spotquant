# USDT market-cap slope: one prospective information screen

Frozen before inspecting the first market-cap response. This is Spotquant-only research. It does not import or copy the unlicensed inspiration repository and does not change main, accounts, schedules or execution.

## Hypothesis and source
A contraction in aggregate USDT circulating supply may indicate weaker dollar-like liquidity for BTC buying even after BTC's own trend and volatility are known. It can also reflect treasury movement, redemptions or measurement revisions; USDT market capitalization is **price × reported circulating supply**, not minting, exchange inflow or capital committed to BTC. The old USDT/USD denomination test and failed same-venue flow/premium routes are different questions; do not treat their price effects as this mechanism.

Source: CoinGecko `/api/v3/coins/tether/market_chart?vs_currency=usd&days=7&interval=daily&precision=full` only when a legal public keyless GET succeeds. The [API reference](https://docs.coingecko.com/demo/reference/coins-id-market-chart) says daily 00:00 UTC data become available about 00:10 UTC and demo history is limited to the past 365 days. Its [revision explanation](https://support.coingecko.com/hc/en-us/articles/61976309053337-Why-do-historical-market-cap-values-change-shortly-after-a-date-then-settle) says historical market cap for a date may change on that date, D+1 and D+2, and has no finality field. Use the actual response receive clock as the earliest **proven** availability; vendor schedule is only a lower bound. A later revised row never overwrites a previously received row.

## Frozen expression and qualification
At each actually eligible manual Spotquant **new external BUY** decision, select the latest immutable source receipt received before that decision. Require three consecutive completed 00:00 UTC daily event points, positive USD market caps and USDT/USD prices, exact cap/price event-time pairing, and receipt at least 10 minutes after the latest event. Reject current intraday/non-midnight points, missing/duplicate points, malformed values, and absent receive/event clocks. Do not mix a today's cap with a stale price or backdate the final historical series. The first receipt is a seed; old rows in it were not observed on their event dates.

For three consecutive days `t-2,t-1,t`, compute the per-day OLS slope of `ln(market_cap_usd / usdt_usd_price)` against offsets 0,1,2; this equals `(ln(S_t)-ln(S_{t-2}))/2`. A strictly negative slope is the sole exposure hypothesis: weaker subsequent BTC outcome than the incumbent's matched positive/flat cases. Zero or positive is its reference. No fitted cut, alternate horizon, event picking or reverse-sign rescue. Report the raw market-cap slope and USDT/USD price slope separately, so a peg move cannot masquerade as supply.

Count one event per actual external BUY order/opportunity, not three sleeves or repeated polling. Require non-overlapping seven-day outcome windows and at least ten mature independent events with at least three in each chronological half. Preserve any event with absent outcome or BTC control as missing. Compare with prior completed BTC momentum/RMS, the incumbent new-BUY decision and participation, USDT/USD movement, identical manual start, and actual known costs. First screen information and real action rate; only if incremental cost-relevant effect survives both halves can one half-budget NEW BUY expression enter a bounded, independently funded account comparison. Held BTC, existing protection, SELL priority, no-topup, order identities and existing crowding remain unchanged.

For an eligible account entrant, compare the incumbent and simple buy/hold or cash and a uniform half-budget control at the same wallet capital, CNY FX, manual sessions, fees/slippage, safety and valuations; include double costs and eras. The original 100% CAGR / 30% drawdown goal remains unmet until directly shown otherwise. The archived replacement-spec numeric return/risk routes are reference gates; the later benefit-harm-policy also requires clear overall net benefit. Historical developer observations cannot be relabeled as prospective.

## Minimum implementation
An on-demand one-request collector stores raw bytes and an append-only metadata receipt with request/receive clocks, status, content hash, predecessor receipt hash and revisions. A small pure parser computes the one frozen slope from receipts already received before a caller-supplied decision time. No recurring task, credential, paid endpoint, Binance request, account read or wallet run.
