# BTC raw block fee-source qualification: one bounded sample

2026-10-06 UTC. Scope: input qualification only for Spotquant research. Historical Coin Metrics `FeeTotNtv/IssTotNtv` was previously blocked by BTC-specific scope, daily-label, and release/vintage uncertainty (`research/search-next-RESULT.md` and its `miner/` archived artifacts). This check uses one different source: a raw BTC mainnet block and transaction obtained with the public, read-only [Blockstream Esplora API](https://github.com/Blockstream/esplora/blob/master/API.md). It neither assumes miner selling nor tests price prediction.

`block-source-spec.json` was written before the selected block was fetched. A preliminary connectivity GET returned tip 970180 and is not used as a qualified receipt. The fixed collection selected the tip reported by its own first recorded GET minus six, yielding height **970174**, hash `000000000000000000000b056858a1f27b8b27df4ae9e1ce056c89c64a23127f`. No block was chosen for its fee or market outcome. Eight planned GETs obtained tip, height hash, block metadata, best-chain status, 80-byte header, coinbase txid, decoded coinbase and raw coinbase hex. The planned eight-request cap mistakenly omitted the Merkle-proof dependency. The original eight-response record is retained locally with SHA-256 `2f7348b339a923c99e9cf4fd57c9e2807f0f807f80285b4cc15b0b1f19296370`; one extra GET fetched **only** that same coinbase's Merkle proof, without reselecting the sample or acquiring more blocks. The final nine-response `block-source-sample.json` SHA-256 is `d49ba8bd7e4936d6a1ad6498ee68f0ed18cf23bc749da67cbefd1a7b9d198263`. Responses include request/receipt UTC, HTTP status, Date and body SHA-256.

Local verification recomputed the block header hash, its encoded UTC header time and Merkle root, the raw SegWit coinbase transaction ID, its integer output values and the coinbase transaction's Merkle inclusion. The provider reported `in_best_chain=true`; this single-provider status is not a full independent chain validation. [Esplora documents](https://github.com/Blockstream/esplora/blob/master/API.md) that its amounts are satoshis. Coinbase outputs were **313,246,875 and 0 satoshis**. Bitcoin's height-based fourth-halving subsidy is **312,500,000 satoshis**. The payout above *maximum allowed subsidy* is **746,875 satoshis** (0.00746875 BTC). This is a **coinbase fee-claim lower bound**, not an independently summed total of the block's 6,659 non-coinbase transaction fees: a miner can underclaim. Bitcoin Core's [`getblockstats` documentation](https://bitcoincore.org/en/doc/26.0.0/rpc/blockchain/getblockstats/) distinguishes `subsidy` and `totalfee`, both in satoshis. No full-block transaction/UTXO scan or Bitcoin Core node was run.

The block header `nTime` was **2026-10-06 12:48:21 UTC**; its median-time-past was **12:02:49 UTC**. The actual public read began **13:48:54.627986 UTC**, the coinbase hex arrived by **13:48:56.695260 UTC**, and the final inclusion proof arrived **13:50:25.408161 UTC**. Only the latter establishes when the *fully verified sample* became available to this process. Header/MTP are consensus ordering fields, not our receipt or the historical publication time of any daily provider metric. A prior historical block fetched now would likewise have only a current receipt.

**Result: one native-BTC, integer-satoshi, proof-linked current observation is qualified at its actual receipt; the old historical daily series is not retroactively qualified.** This raw-block route removes the need to guess Coin Metrics' BTC field scope and day-label convention for *future* per-block observations, but one block gives neither a completed daily statistic nor ten non-overlapping independently received outcome periods. The fixed fee-share family remains a hypothesis about chain settlement demand beyond price and venue trade composition; the measured excess payout does not identify miner selling or predict BTC returns. A daily fee-share signal would require an explicit time bucket based on actually received qualified blocks, separate reconciliation of total fees versus claimed fees, and stable prospective coverage. Those conditions and a simple price/volume/participation control are not yet satisfied, so **NO_ECONOMIC_SCREEN / NO_ACCOUNT / NO_MAIN_CHANGE**. No full-chain collection, historical backfill, threshold search, scheduled capture, paid service, credential, deployment or trading-account action was performed.

Reproduction: run `capture_block_sample.py` once for a new one-block sample, then `capture_proof_addendum.py` once, then `verify_block_sample.py`. These scripts are an archived research aid only; they do not run from `main`. The preserved result here is bound to the exact final raw-response manifest, so rerunning at a later tip would be a new observation, not a reproduction of its timing.
