# Dust entry gate: engineering and economic decisions are separate

The observed paths have distinct purposes:

- `spotquant/follow.py::_owned_fills` marks a receipt-confirmed full SELL remainder as `dust=True`, keeps its actual quantity and `sell_applied`, and calls `model.note_flat()`. A later **already confirmed** BUY fill is merged with that retained ownership. This makes recovery of actual coins explicit.
- `spotquant/session.py::_view` keeps those coins owned and flags the strategy view `_owned_dust`. `spotquant/preview.py::portfolio` checks whether the rounded residual has become placeable; if so it raises `Unknown`. Otherwise it uses zero strategy coins for entry eligibility while charging residual value to exposure.
- `spotquant/preview.py::decision` then checks a proposed new BUY. Its `held()` predicate treats any owned quantity at or above `BASE_STEP` as held even when `dust=True`, `sell_applied` exists, and the residual is still below venue minimum. It removes the unsubmitted BUY. A confirmed residual below `BASE_STEP` passes this second predicate. That is a conservative entry policy inconsistency, not an ownership or balance misposting.

A targeted, synthetic, read-only decision fixture used an armed model, a previously closed receipt-owned dust position, available factor-1 crowding, and an unplaceable residual. With the same holdings, balance, funding, and price, `portfolio()` proposed one BUY both below and above `BASE_STEP`; `decision()` retained the below-step proposal and removed the above-step proposal with `held_sleeve_no_topup`. No venue/account calls or historical wallet replay occurred. Existing grouped-close checks separately cover retained ownership, confirmed terminal SELL before widening dust, placeable-residual `Unknown`, and merge after an actual BUY fill.

The guard is downstream of fill folding and portfolio's external-BTC/placeability checks. In the observed branch it only removes an unsubmitted BUY and its hypothetical protection; it does not cancel an installed stop, mutate persisted positions, reassign fills, erase a pending durable intent, or change recovery. `Lifecycle.act` has no entry order from that decision to send. A proposed one-line change to exempt all verified closed dust would permit extra BTC purchases and materially alter subsequent protection, cap use, risk, and exits. That is a strategy/risk change, even if one can call the two predicates inconsistent. The negative, source-bound price screen and missing per-decision durable position flags do not establish a net benefit or a fully paired safe outcome. **No runtime patch, PR, account run, or main change is justified now.**

Current independent Spot source routes were checked against `research/lifecycle-roadmap.json`, `research/nine-spec.json`, the frozen cross-venue forward protocol, and the current ETF/block-source receipts. The Coinbase/Kraken route has one old development seed with no preregistered result and no mature prospective outcome; its next independent manual observation target is no earlier than 2026-10-12 09:20 UTC, with a valid seven-day endpoint no earlier than 2026-10-19 09:20 UTC if the actual registration occurs at target. ETF has a new complete receipt but no frozen trigger. Raw native BTC block fees have one correctly received proof-linked block, not a qualified completed daily series. Old-coin supply has no receipt-qualified old-coin-spend sample; options and matched OI/liquidation inputs remain immature or incomplete. No currently held independent Spot event set meets the source, maturity, and support gates for another economic variant today. Repeating a same-day capture, changing a trigger, or relabeling old receipts would not add such evidence.
