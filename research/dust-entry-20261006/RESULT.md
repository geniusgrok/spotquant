# Confirmed-close dust entry diagnostic: closed for alpha promotion

The original projection has repeated decision polling. After deduplication, blocked BUY proposals are concentrated in a small number of confirmed SELL ownership states. Each selected last SELL is settled FILLED and its durable executed quantity matches its owned fills. The source `spotquant/follow.py` records a fully closed, unplaceable residual as strategy-flat dust; `spotquant/preview.py` can still veto a new BUY when that residual exceeds the base lot step. This is a credible local guard inconsistency.

The selection, endpoint, fee floor, and stop rule were saved in SPEC.md before the read-only price screen. Running `python research/dust-entry-20261006/dust_entry_screen.py` against the already archived baseline projection produces the aggregate technical counts and screen result. No market data is downloaded, no account is accessed, no wallet is replayed, and repeated polls are not counted as independent trades.

The frozen seven-day price screen did not show a clear gain after even its favorable fee floor and showed an adverse lower tail. It excludes slippage and protection, so the economic case would only weaken under those costs. The projection also omits per-decision durable `dust` and `sell_applied` position flags, and this read-only screen cannot certify the exact live-safe predicate for every historical proposal. Consequently this route is **not qualified for an account variant or main adoption**. This is not a claim that removing the veto would have reduced full-wallet returns; intervening BUYs would change future positions, exits, fees, and risk.

Main remains unchanged. The technical guard issue can be reconsidered separately if a source-bound checkpoint or future authorized observation proves the durable position flags and a paired economic comparison shows net benefit without higher tail risk. Do not infer that the original blocked proposals were realized missed profits.
